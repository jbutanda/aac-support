Best practices

The short version: use the device yourself, out loud, every day. Model more than you ask. Expect the student to have something to say.

Communication Bill of Rights

The rights every person has to communicate, from the National Joint Committee and ASHA.
Communication is a human right. Every student deserves a way to say what they want, what they think, and who they are.
