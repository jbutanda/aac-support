How to use this page: Browse by category or search by keyword. Click Copy to copy any passage to your clipboard. All language is written within a total communication framework: goals and descriptions address communication functions across all modalities, not device use alone. Adjust student name, pronouns, and specific details before using. Brackets [ ] indicate fields to fill in. Every goal opens with a Baseline line: fill it in before you fill in the criterion. Where a passage rests on a specific statute or study, the citation appears beneath it.

Language marked TOTAL COMM is explicitly multimodal in framing. Language marked DEVICE IN PLACE is for students with an individual device or a device trial. Language marked FORMAL EVAL is for students referred for, or following, a formal AT/AAC evaluation (motor, sensory or multidisciplinary needs).
